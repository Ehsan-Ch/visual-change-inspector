Alignment makes a difference

IoU measures overlap between detected and known changed pixels. Both methods use the same accepted shared area and the same threshold. The baseline also receives brightness correction.

Evidence & limits

A controlled experiment

Edits are artificial colored patches. The test uses only two held-out source photographs, with repeated transformations; pairs are not independent real-world inspections.

A single alignment cannot explain parallax, moving shadows, reflections, or non-rigid motion. Quality gates reduce obvious failures but do not guarantee correctness. “No change” applies only to the inspected shared area.
